Privacy Policy

Last updated 10 November 2026

Keepr is a WhatsApp number that saves what you send it and finds it again when you ask. It is operated by Laksh Nijhawan, New Delhi, India. This page says exactly what Keepr stores about you and what it does with it. Keepr is currently in limited testing with a small number of invited people.

What Keepr stores

DataWhy
Your WhatsApp numberTo receive your messages, reply to you, and keep your memories separate from everyone else's.
The words you send: notes, links, captions, forwarding context, and the text of a voice note if voice transcription is switched onThis is the memory itself. It is what you search later.
A short summary and a numeric “meaning” vector of each memorySo a search such as “that thing about coffee” can find it.
Reminders you ask for, with their due timeTo message you when they are due.
When you last messaged Keepr, and whether you sent stopWhatsApp only allows free-form replies for 24 hours after your last message, and we must honour your opt-out.
Timestamps and delivery status of messagesTo avoid saving or answering the same message twice and to fix delivery problems.

Never stored: photos, videos, voice recordings and documents. Only words are kept. A voice recording is turned into text in memory and then discarded; the audio is never written to disk, the database or the logs.

Please do not send card numbers, bank account numbers, government ID numbers (such as Aadhaar, PAN or social security numbers), or other secrets. Keepr is not a vault. It detects the most common card and ID formats and refuses to save them, but it cannot catch everything, so keep secrets in a password manager.

What Keepr does with it

  • Saves, searches and returns your own memories to you, and nothing else.
  • Sends you replies, reminders you asked for, and a short weekly digest on Sunday evenings.
  • It does not sell your data, show ads, build profiles, or use your messages to train AI models.
  • Every save and search is restricted to your own WhatsApp number in the database. Keepr only ever sees messages sent to its own number; it cannot read your other chats or groups.

Messages Keepr sends on its own, and how to stop them

Keepr only writes first for two things: a reminder you set, and the weekly digest. Send stop at any time and both end immediately, and any reminders still waiting are cancelled. Send start to turn them back on. Keepr still answers whenever you message it. Outside WhatsApp's 24-hour reply window these messages use WhatsApp-approved templates, as WhatsApp requires.

Who else handles your data

ServiceRole
WhatsApp / MetaDelivers messages between you and Keepr, under WhatsApp's own terms and privacy policy.
MongoDB AtlasStores your memories, reminders and settings.
VercelHosts the Keepr application.
AI modelsKeepr can run its language models inside its own server, in which case your text is not sent to an AI provider. If that mode is off, the text of a message is sent to Hugging Face to create its summary and meaning vector (and, for voice notes, its transcript).

Keepr does not log message content, phone numbers or access tokens in its application logs.

How long it is kept, and your choices

  • Export: send export to get everything back, with dates, in the chat.
  • Delete in chat: delete followed by a word hides matching memories, and undo within 10 minutes brings them back. Hidden memories are no longer shown or searched, but they remain stored until you ask for permanent deletion below.
  • Permanent deletion: email info@keepr.website from the WhatsApp number you use with Keepr and we will erase your account and every memory stored under it.
  • Otherwise your memories are kept until you ask for them to be deleted or Keepr is shut down.

Security

Incoming webhooks are verified with a cryptographic signature, scheduled jobs and admin routes require secrets, and access to your memories is checked against your number on every request. No system is perfectly secure, so please follow the warning above about secrets. If we learn of a breach affecting you, we will tell you.

Children

Keepr is not intended for anyone under 16, and we do not knowingly store data about them.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will update the date above and tell active users in WhatsApp.

Contact and support

Questions, deletion requests, or help: info@keepr.website. We usually reply within a few days. You can also send help to Keepr on WhatsApp for a list of commands.
