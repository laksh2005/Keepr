Privacy, plainly

This is exactly what Keepr does with your data — nothing more.

  • Media is never stored. Photos, videos, voice notes, and documents are never downloaded or kept. Only the text you write — a caption, a message, forwarding context — is saved.
  • Voice notes aren't transcribed. If you send a voice note with no separate text, Keepr stores a placeholder, not the audio or its content.
  • Your memories are yours alone. Every save and every search is scoped to your WhatsApp number. There is no way for one user's recall to surface another user's memory.
  • No message content is logged. Keepr does not log webhook bodies, captions, tokens, or phone numbers in production.

FAQ

Do I need to install anything?

No. Keepr is a WhatsApp Business number. Save it as a contact and start messaging it.

Can Keepr read my other chats?

No. Keepr only sees messages you send directly to its number.

What happens if I send the same message twice?

Keepr recognizes retried or duplicate deliveries and only saves each message once.

Is this free to use?

Yes, for personal use. Standard WhatsApp messaging rates from your carrier may still apply.
