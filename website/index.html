How it works

One number. Two things it does.

There are no commands to learn and no folders to maintain. You either send Keepr something, or you ask it for something.

STEP 01

Send anything

Forward a link, snap a photo with a caption, drop a PDF, ramble into a voice note, or just type a thought.

STEP 02

Keepr keeps the meaning

It reads your words and writes a short, searchable summary of what the thing was. The media itself is never fetched.

STEP 03

Ask like a human

“that pasta recipe from last week” works. So does “the article about sleep.” No keywords, no tags, no syntax.

STEP 04

Get the original back

Keepr replies as a native WhatsApp quote, so one tap jumps you straight to the message you sent months ago.

Privacy

What Keepr does with your data.

A memory tool only works if you trust it, so here is exactly what gets stored and what never gets touched.

Your media is never stored

Photos, videos, voice notes and documents are never downloaded to our servers. Only the words you write (a caption, a message, forwarding context) are saved.

Voice notes are never transcribed

If you send audio with no accompanying text, Keepr stores a plain placeholder, not the recording and not a transcript of what you said.

Your memories are yours alone

Every save and every search is locked to your WhatsApp number at the database level. There is no code path that can return one person's memory to anyone else.

Message content is never logged

Webhook payloads, captions, access tokens and phone numbers are kept out of application logs in production.

You can have it all deleted

Email us from the number you use with Keepr and we will delete your account and every memory stored under it. No forms, no retention window.

Keepr only ever sees messages you send to its own number. It cannot read your other chats, your groups, or anything else on your phone. WhatsApp does not permit that, and neither does Keepr's design.
FAQ

Common questions.

Do I need to install anything?
No. Keepr is a WhatsApp Business number. Save it as a contact the way you would save a friend, and start messaging it. There is no app, no login, and no browser extension.
Can Keepr read my other conversations?
No. WhatsApp only delivers a business the messages sent directly to that business's own number, so your other chats and groups are invisible to Keepr at the platform level.
What happens if I send the same thing twice?
Each message is stored exactly once. Keepr keys every memory to its original WhatsApp message ID, so duplicate deliveries and network retries can't create clutter.
How does it understand “that thing about coffee”?
Keepr converts every memory into a mathematical representation of its meaning, then compares your question against those. That's why “sleep article” can find something you saved as “circadian rhythm research”. It matches meaning rather than spelling.
What does it cost?
Keepr is free for personal use. Standard messaging rates from your mobile carrier or data plan still apply, exactly as they would for any other WhatsApp chat.
Can I get my data out or deleted?
Yes. Email us from the number registered with Keepr and we will export or permanently delete everything associated with it.
